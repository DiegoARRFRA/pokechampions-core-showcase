## Scope and retained paths

`battle-speed.png` retains its old path for compatibility but now shows speed analysis **inside Versus**, not the former Battle screenshot. `lead-record.png` now really shows the **Lead Trainer Register** tab; it is separate from Battle History, which has its own `history-*` captures.

Practice and match outcomes are manually entered sample data, not independently verified competitive results. Battle represents a situation; it does not autonomously resolve complete matches. Stat stages and outcomes shown in Master Mode apply only to that configured scenario.

Clips are continuous excerpts at original speed with a final held frame; Android bars and audio were removed. No interface, value, warning or waiting period within the selected excerpt was replaced. These edits do not demonstrate calculation correctness or establish a benchmark. 1HITKO damage guarantees do not guarantee move accuracy.
