## Interpreting numbers

The documentary review of 22 September 2026 describes examined dependencies, schema and components. It does not update historical application test results by itself. Each count or measurement retains its date, environment and scope on the relevant page.

## What changes with language

Explanations, navigation, diagram labels and alt text are translated. Table/field names, packages, commands, hashes and paths are preserved. Screenshots retain their recorded interface; translating a page does not change the application shown in it.

Mermaid diagrams are editable documentation. Production source, databases with real records, private catalogues and keys remain outside this showcase.
