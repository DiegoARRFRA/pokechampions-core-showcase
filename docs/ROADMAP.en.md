# Public roadmap

[← Overview](../README.en.md) · [Documentation index](README.en.md)

Product and showcase direction without revealing implementation details or promising delivery dates.

## Available public material

The [gallery](GALLERY.en.md) includes the visual refresh prepared on 22 September 2026: **16 new screenshots and six new demos**, plus the earlier 1HITKO still and demo, clearly labelled. The manifest contains 31 files (17 PNGs and seven GIF/MP4 demos). Media validation is not a new application build or QA campaign.

The [technical overview](TECHNICAL_OVERVIEW.en.md) summarises technologies and execution flow. All showcase Markdown documentation has Spanish and English versions with reciprocal navigation; the [index](README.en.md) lists them.

## Next areas of work

Expand physical Android coverage beyond the primary device; continue accessibility and TalkBack acceptance; complete prolonged audio sessions and investigate device frame pacing; keep regulation-specific data and coverage current without reusing stale validation claims; and improve release notes that connect visible changes with validation checkpoints.

## Product quality

Reduce ambiguous or unsupported mechanical states, preserve deterministic generation and provenance, strengthen migration coverage for long-lived data and expand regressions around cross-feature scenario transfer.

## Engineering showcase

Refresh the earlier 1HITKO material when new recordings are available. Publish additional case studies when they provide useful lessons without exposing private source. Keep ES/EN versions aligned and feedback focused on suggestions, verifiable data corrections and public documentation.

## Outside the default roadmap

PokeChampions Core is not planned as a cloud service, social network, online battle client or autonomous turn simulator. The focus remains local preparation, analysis and review.
